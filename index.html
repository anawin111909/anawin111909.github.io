Player Onboarding Through Progressive Quest Design

Design Summary

The Starter Area is designed as a progressive onboarding experience that introduces the player's core systems step by step. The player first learns to loot items from a treasure chest, then equips their new equipment through the inventory system before entering their first combat encounter. After combat, players learn to manage HP and stamina using consumable items, followed by learning the skill system. Completing the five Starter Quests gives players a foundation in exploration, equipment, combat, resource management, and abilities before progressing to Part 2.
