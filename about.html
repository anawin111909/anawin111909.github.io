I am a Game Designer with a Master's degree in Games Development and Design from the University of Lincoln, with a focus on gameplay and level design.

My background in game testing has also strengthened my approach to design.

I use playtesting, exploratory testing, bug analysis, and player feedback to identify problems, evaluate gameplay experiences, and iterate on my designs.

This allows me to approach game development from both a design and player-focused perspective.
